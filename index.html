What this is. The static General Awareness questions DSSSB has actually asked in its computer-based tests since 2021, sorted by subject. ASO (August 2025) and Patwari (2022) come first, then Junior Assistant and Stenographer (2021), and the General Awareness sections of the PRT, TGT and PGT papers (2023–2026), which DSSSB sets from the same syllabus.

Where the answers come from. Each official DSSSB question paper marks the correct option with a tick. That tick was read straight from the paper — the answers were never typed in by hand. Where the key disagrees with standard textbooks, the question shows a note saying so.

What was left out. Current affairs (events, appointments, awards and reports from the year of the exam), questions whose facts have since changed, and a small number of questions whose text or answer could not be read reliably from the PDF. Questions asked in Hindi are shown in English translation.

No tracking. No network calls. Your answers are saved only in this browser. Press / to search, Esc to close.
