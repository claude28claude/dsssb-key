Functions to remember

  • Improvement and environmental upgradation of JJ bastis and notified slums
  • Rehabilitation / relocation of JJ dwellers, EWS flats
  • Night shelters (rain baseras) and winter tents for the homeless
  • Jan Suvidha Complexes / community toilet complexes, basti vikas kendras
  • Survey of JJ bastis (list of about 675 bastis in the 2015 era)

Policy 2015 vs 2026

  • 2015: basti before 01.01.2006; dweller before 01.01.2015
  • 2015: in-situ or within 5 km; no demolition without alternative housing
  • 2026: basti cut-off 01.01.2025 (via Act amendment), in-situ priority
  • DUSIB's 36th Board meeting extended benefits to settlements up to 01.01.2025

Delhi Act 12 of 1954 · your job's core

Delhi Land Revenue Act, 1954 & land records

Governs revenue officers and courts, record of rights, village maps, boundaries and marks, assessment and recovery of land revenue in rural Delhi. Works together with the Delhi Land Reforms Act, 1954 (tenures). Land revenue is the first charge on land. 7 chapters.

Geography and environment

  • Yamuna enters Delhi at Palla (north) and flows about 48 km south-east; barrages: Wazirabad, ITO, Okhla. Water released from Hathnikund (Haryana)
  • Delhi Ridge: the northern end of the Aravalli range; four parts: Northern (near DU), Central, South-Central (Mehrauli), Southern (Asola)
  • Sahibi river reaches Delhi as the Najafgarh drain, the largest drain into the Yamuna
  • Seismic Zone IV
  • Climate: semi-arid / humid subtropical; hot "loo" winds in May–June; winter rain from western disturbances; monsoon arrives around late June
  • Asola Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary: on the Southern Ridge (Delhi's sanctuary)
  • Delhi has no national park
  • Okhla Bird Sanctuary is in Noida (Uttar Pradesh), not Delhi: a classic trap
  • Yamuna Biodiversity Park, Aravalli Biodiversity Park, Sanjay Van, Najafgarh Jheel
  • National Zoological Park (1959), near Purana Qila
  • Air quality: GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan, 4 stages) enforced by CAQM (Commission for Air Quality Management, Act of 2021)
