River, ridge, climate, green areas

Geography and environment

  • Yamuna enters Delhi at Palla (north) and flows about 48 km south-east; barrages: Wazirabad, ITO, Okhla. Water released from Hathnikund (Haryana)
  • Delhi Ridge: the northern end of the Aravalli range; four parts: Northern (near DU), Central, South-Central (Mehrauli), Southern (Asola)
  • Sahibi river reaches Delhi as the Najafgarh drain, the largest drain into the Yamuna
  • Seismic Zone IV
  • Climate: semi-arid / humid subtropical; hot "loo" winds in May–June; winter rain from western disturbances; monsoon arrives around late June
  • Asola Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary: on the Southern Ridge (Delhi's sanctuary)
  • Delhi has no national park
  • Okhla Bird Sanctuary is in Noida (Uttar Pradesh), not Delhi: a classic trap
  • Yamuna Biodiversity Park, Aravalli Biodiversity Park, Sanjay Van, Najafgarh Jheel
  • National Zoological Park (1959), near Purana Qila
  • Air quality: GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan, 4 stages) enforced by CAQM (Commission for Air Quality Management, Act of 2021)

Anti-corruption machinery

Vigilance function in Delhi

  • Directorate of Vigilance (GNCTD) works under the Chief Secretary, who is the Chief Vigilance Officer of the NCT
  • Headed by the Director (Vigilance), who is also ex-officio Joint Commissioner of Police, Anti-Corruption Branch (ACB)
  • Vigilance wing: disciplinary cases and complaints against government employees (from the public, CBI, CVC)
  • ACB: traps, raids and investigation under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988
  • Nodal advisor to the vigilance officers of departments and local bodies
  • Delhi Lokayukta and Upalokayukta Act, 1995 (Delhi Act 1 of 1996): Lokayukta inquires into allegations against public functionaries (misuse of position, lack of integrity, disproportionate assets)
  • CVC (Central Vigilance Commission, statutory under the CVC Act 2003) supervises vigilance in central bodies
  • Vigilance Awareness Week is held in the week of 31 October (Sardar Patel's birth anniversary)
  • Since the 2023 GNCTD amendment, postings and vigilance matters of officers go through the National Capital Civil Service Authority (CM, Chief Secretary, Principal Secretary Home)

Water and sewerage

Delhi Jal Board (DJB)

  • Set up under the Delhi Jal Board Act, 1998, replacing the Delhi Water Supply & Sewage Disposal Undertaking of the MCD
  • Functions: drinking water supply, sewerage, sewage treatment and disposal, drainage
  • Chairperson is usually the Water Minister; a CEO runs it
  • Free water up to 20 kilolitres a month for households (since 2015)
  • Raw water: Yamuna (via Haryana: Wazirabad, Munak canal), Ganga (Upper Ganga Canal → Sonia Vihar, Bhagirathi plants), Bhakra storage, groundwater
  • Water treatment plants: Haiderpur, Chandrawal, Wazirabad, Sonia Vihar, Bhagirathi, Okhla, Nangloi, Bawana, Dwarka
  • Okhla sewage treatment plant: 564 MLD, Asia's largest; fully working from 2025; built with the National Mission for Clean Ganga

Computer Advance

Email and communication

  • First email: Ray Tomlinson, 1971 (ARPANET); he chose the @ sign
  • Address = username@domain (e.g. name@gov.in)
  • Fields: To, Cc (carbon copy), Bcc (blind carbon copy, hidden), Subject, Body, Attachment, Signature
  • Actions: Reply, Reply All, Forward; folders: Inbox, Outbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam/Junk, Trash
  • Protocols: SMTP sends (port 25/587), POP3 downloads (110), IMAP syncs (143), MIME for attachments and multimedia
  • Gmail attachment limit 25 MB (larger files go via Drive link)
  • Services: Hotmail (1996, Sabeer Bhatia & Jack Smith; now Outlook.com), Yahoo Mail (1997), Gmail (2004), Rediffmail (Indian), NIC email for government
  • Threats: spam, phishing, email spoofing; use 2FA
  • Synchronous (live: calls, video meetings, chat) vs asynchronous (email, forums)
  • Tools: instant messaging (WhatsApp 2009), VoIP (Skype 2003), video conferencing (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams), SMS/MMS, fax
  • Short range: Bluetooth (about 10 m), NFC (a few cm, tap-to-pay), Wi-Fi
